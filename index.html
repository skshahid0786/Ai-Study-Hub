    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers before you start.

    Is the AI always correct?

    No. AI can write wrong answers with full confidence. Always check important facts against your textbook, notes or teacher, especially before exams.

    Can I use this for patients?

    No. This is a study tool only. It must never be used to diagnose or treat real patients.

    Why did I see a limit or error message?

    Free AI plans have daily and per-minute limits. The site tries its other keys automatically. If all hit their limit, wait a little and try again.

    Where is my history saved?

    Only in this browser on this device. If you clear browser data, the history is removed.

    Is it safe to share this page?

    The API keys are written inside this file. Anyone who gets the file can read and use them. Keep it for your own use and do not upload it to a public website.
